# PRODUCT.md — Prime Golden Properties

## Register
Brand / marketing site. Design IS the product: a visitor's impression of trust and quality is the thing being made. Lead surface is the homepage.

## What / Who / Why
- **What:** Prime Golden Properties (PGP) sells DTCP/CMDA-approved residential **plots** and **turnkey construction** in the Cheyyar Taluk corridor, ~100 km south-west of Chennai, Tamil Nadu.
- **Who:** Tamil families (local + NRI, esp. Singapore/Gulf) buying land as a long-term, legacy/retirement asset; price-sensitive but documentation- and trust-driven.
- **Why:** The category is full of opaque brokers. PGP's wedge is radical transparency — full legal due-diligence folder before any advance, published per-sq-ft construction rates, one team end-to-end.

## Brand personality
Three words: **rooted, honest, quietly premium.** Trustworthy and grounded (land, soil, family), not flashy. "Right Location. Right Decision." Tagline in use: *Land that turns into legacy.*

## Anti-references
- Generic AI-SaaS landing template (gradient blobs, Inter, glassmorphism for its own sake).
- Loud brokerage "MEGA OFFER" real-estate aesthetic.
- Editorial-magazine serif/italic/drop-cap lane (wrong register for land).

## Committed visual identity (identity-preservation — do not second-guess)
- **Color:** deep forest green `#0F3D2E` (primary/voice), gold `#D4A017` (accent), warm cream `#F8F5EF` (light surface). Green-drenched is on-brand.
- **Type:** Sora (display/headings), Plus Jakarta Sans (body). Both already shipping.
- **Motion:** Framer Motion. One orchestrated page-load > scattered micro-interactions.
- **Imagery:** real photography of plotted layouts, construction, families on the land (in `public/images/brand/`). Land brief → imagery is required, not optional.

## Stack
Next.js 16 (App Router, Turbopack), Tailwind v4, Framer Motion, lucide-react icons. Deployed on Netlify.
